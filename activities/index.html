Activities

Every activity DreamSpace Academy has recorded, dated, one page each.

On this page 202620252024202320222021202020192018

This is the record of what happened: workshops, bootcamps, openings, visits, festivals and relief operations, one page each, in the order they occurred. It is added to rather than finished.

The pages under about the organisation describe how the work is meant to fit together; these pages are the record it can be held against, including the days where the entry is a date, a place and a photograph and nothing more.

How to read the numbers on these pages

Where a participant count appears it is a count of people in a room on a day, recorded at the time. It is not a count of separate individuals, and the counts must never be added together into a figure for reach: a repeated title here means the same format delivered again to a different group, and summing five mornings into one number would overstate the reach.

What this record does not yet hold

The record runs from the founding in September 2018 to the present, and it is not complete. It is built from what was documented at the time, photographs, posts, planning packs , so a quiet month here means a month nobody wrote down, not a month with nothing in it. Of the 292 activities listed above, 155 carry images and the rest show a placeholder while their pictures are prepared for publication.

Where a page says the record holds only a date and a place, that is the record and not an editing choice. A thin entry that is true is published rather than a full one that is partly invented, and thin entries are the ones most likely to be filled in later. Two entries name a partner that could not be independently confirmed, and say so on the page rather than dropping the work.

The unevenness across years is real. 2021 holds three logged deliveries and a launch to the International Space Station; 2024 holds sixty-eight entries. That is a pandemic, an economic crisis and a recovery, and it is not smoothed into a growth curve.
